How do I get protein per box?

Paste or type your ingredients, cook, then weigh the finished food. If you know your empty pot's weight, weigh the full pot; if not, choose "I forgot to weigh the empty pot" or weigh the food on its own. No scale? Use the planned amount and Foreweigh marks the numbers as estimates.

Do I need a kitchen scale?

No, but it makes the numbers exact. Without one, Foreweigh uses the planned amounts and labels them "about".

How do I add food I didn't cook?

On Today, tap "Add food". Search for it, scan the barcode on the package, or enter its protein and calories yourself.

Apple Health isn't receiving my meals

Open Profile and turn on "Save what you eat to Apple Health". If Foreweigh says it isn't allowed, open Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices › Foreweigh and turn on the nutrition types.

Restore or cancel Foreweigh Plus

Restore: open the Plus screen and tap "Restore". Cancel: Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Foreweigh. Your recipes and fridge stay free either way.
