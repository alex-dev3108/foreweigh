Foreweigh for iPhone

Weigh the pot once. Know the protein in every box all week.

Foreweigh is a meal-prep companion. Paste a recipe, cook it, weigh the finished pot, and Foreweigh splits it into portions with protein, calories, carbs and fat for each. Then tap "Eat 1" from your fridge all week, with an optional copy in Apple Health.
