Foreweigh

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

In short: Foreweigh keeps everything you enter on your iPhone. There are no accounts and no ads, and your data is never sold or shared. Two things leave the phone: a barcode number when you scan one, and anonymous counts of which steps people use, with nothing about you or your food in them.

What Foreweigh stores

Your recipes, cooks, pot weights, portions, what you eat, the foods you add, your goals, reminder times and settings. All of it is stored on your iPhone, in the app's own storage, and backed up to your own private iCloud unless you turn that off (see iCloud backup below). It is not sent to us or to anyone else.

Apple Health

If you turn on "Save what you eat to Apple Health", Foreweigh writes each portion you eat and each food you add to Apple Health as a food entry with protein, calories, carbs and fat. Foreweigh only writes to Health; it never reads your Health data. Health data is never used for advertising or marketing, never shared with third parties, and never stored outside your iPhone by Foreweigh. You can turn this off in the app, or remove Foreweigh's access in the Health app at any time.

Barcode lookups

If you scan or type a barcode, Foreweigh sends only that barcode number to Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org), a free, open food database, to get the product's nutrition label. Nothing else is sent: no account, no identifier, no history. Like any request over the internet, it reaches Open Food Facts from your phone's IP address; Foreweigh doesn't keep that address, and Open Food Facts' own privacy policy covers how they handle it. Product data is © Open Food Facts contributors and is used under the Open Database License (ODbL). Food search works offline and sends nothing. The camera is used only while the barcode screen, the nutrition-label scan or the share-a-cook photo is open. A label is read on your iPhone and its picture isn't kept. Barcode images are not kept; a photo you take for a share card stays on your iPhone and is only used in the picture you choose to share.

iCloud backup

So you don't lose your data when you change iPhones, Foreweigh keeps a copy of your recipes, cooks, food log, goals and app settings in your own iCloud, in the private database Apple provides to each app. It is tied to the Apple Account on your iPhone, only you can access it, and the developer of Foreweigh cannot see or read it. When you set up Foreweigh on a new iPhone signed in to the same Apple Account, the app offers to bring it back. You can turn iCloud backup off, back up or restore at any time in Profile › Settings › Data and privacy. Erasing all Foreweigh data there also deletes this backup. Apple's handling of iCloud data is covered by Apple's own privacy policy.

Anonymous usage counts

To learn which parts of the app help, Foreweigh sends anonymous counts to TelemetryDeck, a service built and hosted in Germany. Each count is the name of a step (for example "batch weighed" or "paywall seen"), the app version and, if you answered them, the two onboarding labels (such as "cut" and "every week") or your answer to the "how would you feel" question. It's tied to a random code made on your iPhone, hashed before it's sent and hashed again by TelemetryDeck, so it can't be linked to you. No food, weights, goals, names, contacts or Health data are ever sent.

Notifications

If you turn on meal reminders, Foreweigh schedules them on your iPhone. Nothing is sent to a server.

Recipe screenshots

If you import a recipe from a screenshot, the text is read on your iPhone. The image is not uploaded or kept.

Subscriptions

Foreweigh Plus is sold through the App Store. Apple handles payment; we never see your payment details. The app checks your subscription with Apple on your device.

Exporting and deleting your data

In Profile › Settings › Data and privacy you can export everything Foreweigh stores, or erase it all. Deleting the app also deletes its data. Erasing data does not cancel a subscription; manage that in your Apple Account settings.

Your rights

Your data stays on your iPhone and, if you keep iCloud backup on, in your own private iCloud, so the developer of Foreweigh never receives your personal data; the anonymous counts above can't identify you. A barcode lookup happens only when you ask for one, to give you the product's label. Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct, delete and export your data; in Foreweigh you can do all of this yourself in Profile › Settings › Data and privacy. You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority. In Romania that is ANSPDCP (dataprotection.ro).

Children

Foreweigh is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone.
